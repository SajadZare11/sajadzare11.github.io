Biography & Journey

Where Linguistics Meets Artificial Intelligence

Born in 1999 · Educator at NODET · Dual Master's in AI and Technology-Assisted Language Learning.

I am Sajad Zare (born in 1999), an educator and technologist standing at the intersection of language pedagogy and computational intelligence. My academic journey began with a B.A. in English Language Teaching from Shahid Beheshti University of Mashhad, instilling in me deep linguistic fundamentals and teaching methodologies.

To push the boundaries of modern education, I pursued dual graduate studies: earning an M.Sc. in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence, alongside an M.A. in English Language Teaching specializing in Technology-Assisted Language Learning (CALL).

Since 2018, I have been teaching English across diverse academic stages. Since 2020, I have had the honor of teaching at Shahid Beheshti High School for Exceptional Talents (NODET / SAMPAD) in Torbat-e Heydarieh, guiding brilliant young minds toward academic excellence, high exam rankings, and language mastery.

Since 2024, I have been coding with Python to develop software tools, and since 2025, my primary research focus has centered on the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence in English Language Teaching (AI in ELT)—designing intelligent learning environments, automated feedback pipelines, and adaptive pedagogies.

"Technology shouldn't replace the teacher—it should amplify human potential. By combining Artificial Intelligence with pedagogical precision, we can unlock unprecedented fluency for every learner."
